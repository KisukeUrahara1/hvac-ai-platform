Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

HVAC AI Platform provides AI-powered communication and lead notification services for HVAC businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information necessary to operate the service, including:

  • Business contact information
  • Names and phone numbers
  • Customer call information
  • Service locations
  • HVAC service requests and problem descriptions
  • Call summaries and lead information
  • SMS notification preferences

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and operate the HVAC AI Platform, including answering business calls, identifying potential HVAC service leads, generating call summaries, and sending operational lead notifications to authorized business users.

SMS Communications

Business users may choose to receive SMS notifications containing operational information about qualified HVAC leads.

Message frequency varies depending on the number of incoming customer calls and qualified leads. Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Recipients may reply HELP for help.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing or using the underlying service.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information may be provided to service providers only when necessary to operate the messaging service, deliver requested communications, maintain the platform, prevent fraud, or comply with applicable law.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Service Providers

We may use technology and communications service providers to operate the platform, process calls, provide AI functionality, host infrastructure, and deliver SMS messages. These providers receive information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your Choices

Business users may request changes to their contact information or SMS notification preferences. SMS recipients may stop future text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
